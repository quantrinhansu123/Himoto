# Refund recipient from QR / bank screenshot

The return dialog previously accepted a saved QR as evidence but required users to type recipient fields themselves. In **Thu phí và hoàn cọc**, select the actual refund decision, then **Tải ảnh QR để tự điền** or **Chụp ảnh QR**. A saved QR can also be read again.

- Uploads save into the selected vehicle's existing return document-2 evidence slot. An upload never records a return, refund voucher or Telegram notification.
- Decode NAPAS account-transfer QR locally with jsQR and CRC validation. Keep the account as a string, including leading zeros. Ignore QR amounts and reject card transfers, unknown bank codes, malformed/duplicate TLV fields and invalid checksums.
- Gemini copies the bank, account and owner name printed on the image. Decoded QR bank/account take precedence. Disagreeing printed account/bank cannot provide an owner name. Missing owner names remain blank; they are not resolved through a bank account lookup.
- Preserve manual changes made during a read. A newly scanned account does not inherit the previous account's bank or owner name.
- Block confirmation while saving/reading. Save errors and AI errors remain visible with retry/manual input available. A decodable QR still works when AI is unavailable.
- Dedicated `POST /api/auth/order/car-rental/:id/refund-qr` uses `contracts.return`, same-origin/session checks and the existing scoped order query before any AI call. Existing customer OCR permissions are unchanged. Responses are private/no-store; credentials stay on the server.
